      How to read the game

      Truth zones
      Each end zone holds the lemmas the Lean checker has proved on that side. Inside, every lemma stands in front of what it rests on, so foundations sit at the back.
      Grey field
      Open lemmas, placed by our guess at how likely each is true: farther toward its zone means likelier. The guess uses the plan (a planned chain step leans true), citations, failed attempts and corrections. It will be wrong sometimes; a lemma that gets checked runs into the zone.
      The ball
      The claim itself, at midfield. It moves into an end zone only on a proof. Lines run from each program's target to the ball: solid when the target would settle the claim, dashed with what is missing when it would not.
      Score
      Points are program targets proved: none yet. First downs are checked steps on a program's chain toward its target.
      The stands
      Meta-complexity and known results pull neither way, so they sit along the sidelines. So do the snipers: a kill is recorded by proving a lemma's exact opposite, and that twin fires the laser.
      Coaches and players
      Coaches sit with their team's score: well-known experts on record in each camp; tap a face for the quote and its source. Players are the most active on each side: the fleet's orchestrators and outside contributors, with the record's counts.
