How to read the field

Left or right
The answer the lemma's program aims at, as the record states it. The P = NP algorithm search is on the left. Geometric complexity theory, circuit lower bounds and proof complexity are on the right. The library of known results and meta-complexity are marked neutral, so they sit in the grey area.
Distance from midfield
How much the program's plan leans on the lemma. From the middle out: known results, helpers, lemmas other lemmas rest on, rungs on the program's chain, the hard link it is stuck on, and its target in the end zone. This ranking is this page's rule, not the project's.
Height
When the lemma's status last changed, Pacific time, newest at the top. The record starts on Nov 22.
Shape
Status. The Lean checker decides "checked", with no partial credit. A refuted lemma is drawn as a node killed by a counterexample: its exact opposite was checked. It stays on its own side, because it ends an attempt there and adds nothing to the other side.
Outer ring
A rough attention signal: the project's own news covered the lemma, or a paper claims it. Attempt counts show in the details for lemmas on the two sides.
Lines
"Rests on" links from the record: the lemma is proved assuming the one it points to. There are few of them; most lemmas stand alone.
