How to read the field

The claim
The knot at midfield where every rope ties in. No lemma in the record states P ≠ NP; P = NP appears as the P = NP search's goal, "SAT is in P", which is equivalent to it. The knot slides with the toy model below the play bar but stays in the grey area, since only a proof could pull it out. Tap it for the bridges and barriers.
Sides
The answer a lemma is evidence for. Right: geometric complexity theory, proof complexity, circuit lower bounds, and the lower bounds the P = NP search used to kill its own candidate algorithms. Left: the P = NP search's goal and its candidate algorithms. The grey middle holds meta-complexity and known results, which pull neither way.
Ropes
One row per theme, plus one for each program's chain toward its target. Theme rules are keyword matches over the site's search index.
Distance from midfield
Strength. Open claims stand at the front. Checked lemmas stand farther back, and farther still the more lemmas rest on them. A lemma always stands in front of what it rests on, so a chain reads as a stack from its target at the front to its foundations at the back.
Color
Activity: gold when just proposed or settled, cooling to dull over hours of quiet. Dark grey is out of play: killed (with a cross), or faded support whose only use was killed or withdrawn.
Size
Criticality: plan role (target, hard link, chain step) plus everything that rests on it. Or effort: attempts recorded, available for the two sides only.
Lasers
Kills. The project refutes a lemma by checking its exact negation, its twin. Twins stand as snipers in the opposing end zone and pull nothing. Blue lasers kill P ≠ NP evidence, red lasers kill P = NP evidence, white ones fall in the grey middle. Lemmas a kill rests on stay in play.
Data: the public export at data.pequalnp.com. An independent view of public data. Not made by or affiliated with pequalnp.com. Plan links follow each program's own list of steps and are not proofs; solid links are the record's proven "rests on" links.
