How to read the roots

The surface
Each program's target sits just under it, drawn dashed while unproved. A target breaks the surface when Lean checks it. None has yet. P vs NP itself is the sign in the sky, far above any one target.
Taproot
The program's chain of steps toward its target, in the order its plan lists them. Solid where checked, dashed where open. The gap at the top is the hard link the program is stuck on. The chain is the plan's own list; only some steps are formally linked.
Side roots
One per theme, grouped by words in the lemmas (rules in the table below). Roots grow at the tips, so the oldest lemmas sit near the taproot and the newest at the ends. Root thickness grows with the number checked.
Bedrock
The library of known results, rebuilt in Lean so the programs stand on checked ground.
Color and shape
Blue roots aim at P = NP, red at P ≠ NP, brown are neutral. A filled node is checked, a ring is open, and a struck-through ring was killed by a counterexample.
