Controls

Touch
put a finger on the sky: your dragon turns toward it · lift to fly straight · bolt button (or a second finger) to boost · chevrons to brake · flame for dragonfire · ❚❚ to pause
Mouse
steer — the further from centre, the harder you turn
Click / W / Space
boost (burns length)
Right-click / S
brake — tighter turns
A / D
roll
Arrow keys
steer without the mouse
C
look back (also: wide view & mirror in Settings → Player)
Body temp
the sun at the centre and crowds warm you up — hotter = faster, with a bigger thrust tank
Thrust
boost runs on a ~10 s tank that refills when you're not boosting
E / F
breathe dragonfire — grab a red crystal first; cuts the first tail in the cone
Esc / P
pause — online, folds your wings: after 3 s you leave the sky and your length is kept
N
music on / off
M
mute all sound
